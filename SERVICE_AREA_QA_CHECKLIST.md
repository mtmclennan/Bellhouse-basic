# Bellhouse Service Area Page QA Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing or approving any Bellhouse service-area page.

## Page Identity

- [ ] The page has a unique title tag for the target city.
- [ ] The page has a unique meta description for the target city.
- [ ] The title uses the primary city and real excavation/site-work intent naturally.
- [ ] The meta description reads clearly and is not stuffed with repeated keywords.
- [ ] The page has one unique H1 that matches the city and page angle.

## Localized Content

- [ ] The intro is localized to the city or service area, not just city-swapped template copy.
- [ ] The intro reflects real local conditions, such as lot type, access, schedule pressure, slopes, drainage, rural context, redevelopment context, or active site flow where relevant.
- [ ] Service descriptions are localized and materially different from other city pages.
- [ ] Service descriptions stay within Bellhouse's real scope:
  - excavation
  - site preparation
  - grading
  - dump truck hauling
  - material delivery
  - equipment floating
- [ ] The "Who we work with" section is present and specific to the kinds of clients and crews common in that area.
- [ ] The "Why choose Bellhouse" section is present and explains practical local advantages, not generic marketing language.

## Links And Navigation

- [ ] The page includes useful internal links to relevant core service pages.
- [ ] Internal link anchor text is descriptive and not generic filler such as "click here" or "view related service."
- [ ] The nearby areas section is present.
- [ ] Nearby service-area links are included where matching pages exist.
- [ ] Nearby areas are relevant to the city and support real service-area navigation.

## FAQ

- [ ] The FAQ section is present on the page.
- [ ] FAQ questions answer real local service questions, not generic filler.
- [ ] FAQ content covers excavation and trucking/material coordination where relevant.
- [ ] FAQ content covers who Bellhouse works with where useful.
- [ ] FAQ content covers project-fit or quote-fit questions where useful.
- [ ] FAQ answers are clear, practical, and aligned with the visible page content.

## CTA

- [ ] The page has a clear primary CTA.
- [ ] CTA language is direct and action-oriented.
- [ ] CTA language matches Bellhouse's actual offer, such as quote requests or project discussions.
- [ ] CTA wording is consistent with the rest of the service-area system.

## Content Risks

- [ ] There is no placeholder text.
- [ ] There is no meta commentary, draft language, or notes to future editors.
- [ ] There is no fake-local wording that implies Bellhouse has a staffed office, yard, or branch in the target city unless that is actually true.
- [ ] The page honestly presents the city as a service area, not a branch location.
- [ ] The copy does not overclaim services Bellhouse does not provide.

## Metadata And Schema

- [ ] Canonical URL matches the real page URL.
- [ ] Open Graph title and description align with the page's actual local angle.
- [ ] Structured data reflects one real business serving the area, not a fake office in the city.
- [ ] FAQ schema matches the visible FAQ content exactly.

## Images

- [ ] Images support the section they appear in.
- [ ] Images feel jobsite-relevant, not decorative filler.
- [ ] Alt text is descriptive and natural.
- [ ] Alt text does not stuff city names unnaturally.

## Final Pass

- [ ] The page is materially different from other service-area pages.
- [ ] The page reads like it was written for this service area specifically.
- [ ] The page is clear enough that a contractor, builder, or property owner can tell whether Bellhouse is a fit.
