# Bellhouse Service-Area QA Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing or approving any Bellhouse service-area page.

## Metadata

- [ ] The title tag is unique to the city.
- [ ] The meta description is unique to the city.
- [ ] The title uses the city and excavation/site-work intent naturally.
- [ ] The meta description reads clearly and does not stack repeated keywords.

## Core Structure

- [ ] The page has one unique H1.
- [ ] The page follows the approved structure:
  - Hero
  - Local intro
  - Core services
  - Why choose Bellhouse
  - Who it's for
  - FAQ
  - CTA
- [ ] No extra sections were added just to carry weak or repetitive copy.
- [ ] Each section has one clear purpose only.

## Content Quality

- [ ] The page does not repeat the same value proposition across hero, intro, services, why choose, FAQ, and CTA.
- [ ] The page has strong local specificity.
- [ ] The intro reflects real local conditions such as slopes, drainage, rural lots, redevelopment pressure, active site flow, tighter access, driveway runs, or staging constraints where relevant.
- [ ] Service descriptions are localized and materially different from other service-area pages.
- [ ] The copy stays within Bellhouse's real scope:
  - excavation
  - site preparation
  - grading
  - dump truck hauling
  - material delivery
  - equipment floating

## Who It's For

- [ ] The "Who it's for" section is present.
- [ ] The section clearly explains which jobs, clients, or crews are a good fit.
- [ ] The section helps a builder, contractor, or property owner decide whether Bellhouse fits the job.

## CTA

- [ ] The page has a clear primary CTA.
- [ ] CTA language is direct, practical, and contractor-focused.
- [ ] CTA wording matches Bellhouse's real offer, such as requesting a quote, calling, or texting about the project.
- [ ] CTA copy does not feel vague, soft, or generic.

## FAQ

- [ ] The FAQ section is present on the page.
- [ ] FAQ questions answer real customer questions.
- [ ] FAQ answers are complete, practical, and useful.
- [ ] FAQ content covers excavation and trucking/material coordination where relevant.
- [ ] FAQ content covers fit, scope, or who Bellhouse works with where useful.
- [ ] FAQ content aligns with the visible on-page FAQ markup.

## Internal Links

- [ ] Internal links to relevant core service pages are present.
- [ ] Nearby-area links are present where matching pages exist.
- [ ] Internal link anchor text is descriptive and useful.
- [ ] Internal links support navigation without feeling forced or overused.

## Risk Checks

- [ ] There is no placeholder text.
- [ ] There is no meta commentary or internal wording about the page, section, template, or content strategy.
- [ ] There is no fake local-office wording.
- [ ] The page does not imply Bellhouse has a staffed office, branch, or yard in the city unless that is actually true.
- [ ] The city is presented honestly as a service area.

## Final Pass

- [ ] The page feels written for this city, not swapped from another page.
- [ ] The page is sharp, scannable, and conversion-focused.
- [ ] The page reads like a local excavation landing page, not a long stacked content page.
